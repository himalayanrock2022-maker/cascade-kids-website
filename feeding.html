Feeding Therapy

For picky eaters, sensory feeders, and children who struggle at mealtimes.

🍽️

What we help with

Picky eating, food refusal, sensory aversions, chewing / swallowing, mealtime behaviors, and expanding food variety — with a family-centered, pressure-free approach.

👩‍⚕️

How it works

OT + Speech + Behavior team together. Assessment, mealtime plan, parent coaching, and home practice. We work with your pediatrician and dietitian where needed.

❤️

No-pressure approach

We build trust around food first. No force-feeding, no battles — small wins, step by step.
