About Us

We don't fix kids. We help them grow into who they already are.

Our Story

Cascade Kids started in Seattle with one idea — families should not wait months for help, and should not run to 4 different places for ABA, speech and OT. One team, one plan, one place.

Our Team

BCBAs, RBTs, SLPs and OTs working together. Small caseloads, so your child is not a number.

Why Us

  • No waitlist — most families start in 2 weeks
  • BCBA-led, small caseloads
  • In-home, school, daycare & center options
  • Neurodiversity-affirming, assent-based care
