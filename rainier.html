Rainier Early Start Program

Our early intensive program for ages 2-6 — play-based, goal-driven intervention in the most important developmental window.

🌱

What is it?

Intensive early intervention combining ABA + Speech + OT. Small ratios, play-based learning, and daily parent coaching.

🎯

Goals

Communication, social engagement, regulation, early learning readiness, independence — the foundations for school.

📈

Who is it for?

Children ages 2-6 with autism, developmental delays, or early concerns. Early is better — the earlier we start, the stronger the gains.
