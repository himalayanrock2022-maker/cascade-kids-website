Our Services

One team for your child — ABA, speech, OT and early help, all coordinated together.

🌲

ABA Therapy

Evidence-based ABA for autism and developmental needs. BCBA designs the plan, RBT delivers it, parents are trained every week. Focus on communication, independence, social skills and school readiness.

🗣️

Speech Therapy

Speech-language therapy for late talkers, speech delays, apraxia, and autism-related communication. From first words to full conversations.

🧤

Occupational Therapy

Sensory processing, feeding, fine motor, self-care and classroom participation — practical help for everyday life.

🌧️

Rainier Early Start Program

Our early intensive program for ages 2–6. Play-based, goal-driven intervention in the most important developmental window.

🤝

Social Skills Groups

Small, guided peer groups in Seattle to practice friendship, turn-taking, flexibility and group play.

🏠

Parent Coaching & School Support

We join IEP meetings, teacher meetings and school observations — so progress follows your child everywhere.
